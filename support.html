Deduction Isles — Support

Contact

Email rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com. It helps to include your device model, your iOS or macOS version, and the case number shown on the chart.

I think a case has no valid answer

Every case is checked before it ships: exactly one claim is impossible, and the contradiction is provable from what the board shows you. If you believe a case cannot be solved, send the case number and what you had ruled out. That is a genuine bug report and it is useful.

How the daily case works

A new case appears each day and is free, permanently. The chart archive — every past case — is a one-time purchase. Buying the archive is optional; the daily case does not require it.

Access and purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple and tied to your Apple Account. If you reinstall the app or set up a new device, use Restore Purchases on the archive screen to bring the unlock back. If a restore does not work, email the address above with the Apple Account region you purchased in; refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored on the device, not on a server, so it does not follow you between devices and is removed if the app is deleted. There is no account to sign into, which is also why nothing about your play is ever sent anywhere.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and contains no networking code. Full detail on the privacy policy page.
