Deduction Isles — Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

The short version

Deduction Isles does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking. Every case is bundled inside the app and solved on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Game progress, preferences, board state, and the access-window start date are stored on your device. A small Today widget snapshot is stored in an Apple App Group that is accessible only to Deduction Isles and its widget. The developer does not receive this information.

Network access — stated precisely

The app contains no networking code. It does not open connections, and there is no developer-operated server for it to contact. The only network activity associated with Deduction Isles is Apple's own handling of in-app purchases, described below.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Deduction Isles receives the on-device entitlement status needed to unlock the chart archive, but the developer does not receive your payment details and does not send transaction data to a developer-operated server.

Sharing

When you choose Share, Deduction Isles creates an image on your device and opens the system share sheet. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination, and the app does not receive a copy.

Children

Deduction Isles collects no personal data from anyone, including children. There is no account, no messaging, no user-generated content, and no advertising.

Changes

If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before that version is released.
